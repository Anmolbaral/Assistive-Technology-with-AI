## Why AI for AT Resources?

The challenge is real: many K-12 teachers receive little formal training in assistive technology, yet they're expected to support diverse learners every day. Finding the right AT tools can feel overwhelming—sifting through countless websites, vendor materials, and research studies takes time educators don't have.

**The opportunity:** AI can dramatically reduce the time spent finding relevant, evidence-based AT resources. Instead of hours of searching, you can get personalized recommendations in seconds.

**TechBridge Learning's commitment:** We believe AI should empower educators while protecting student privacy. Our AI assistant is built on a foundation of responsible practices, transparent citations, and educator-centered design.
