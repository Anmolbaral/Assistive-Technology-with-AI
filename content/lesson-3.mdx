<InfoCard title="What is PII?" icon="Shield" variant="warning">
**Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** is any data that could identify a specific student. Under FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) and COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), educators must protect student PII from unauthorized disclosure.
</InfoCard>

## Safe Query Checklist

Before you submit a query to the AI assistant, ask yourself:

- [ ] **Could this identify a specific student?**  
  (If yes, remove names, IDs, or specific details)

- [ ] **Am I describing a general challenge or context?**  
  (If no, make it more general)

- [ ] **Would I feel comfortable posting this publicly?**  
  (If no, it probably contains PII—rephrase it)

- [ ] **Does this include the SETT elements (Student need, Environment, Task, Tools)?**  
  (If yes, great! You're providing helpful context without PII)

## What Happens if I Accidentally Include PII?

Don't panic! TechBridge Learning's AI assistant has built-in PII detection. If your query contains potential PII, you'll receive:

1. **A privacy reminder** (no query is processed)
2. **A specific hint** about what to remove
3. **A chance to rephrase and resubmit**

This safety layer protects you and your students.
